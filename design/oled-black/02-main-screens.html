Main screens — Direction A+C

The six fixed navigation destinations in the agreed language: A's typographic restraint with C's generous spacing. OLED-black, hairline dividers, cards used only where they group an interaction (quests, hobbies, sleep check-in), monochrome outline icons, and color only on live +/− values. PROPOSAL marks a visual decision on an item the brief left open.

Decisions

  • Four distinct day states — gain (green), loss (red), zero (faint 0), unreported (faint –). A missing day is never shown as success or failure.
